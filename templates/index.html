Step 06

Cosine Similarity in N-Dimensional Space

Computes the normalized dot product (cosine of the angle θ) between document vectors.

$$\text{Cosine Similarity}(\vec{A}, \vec{B}) = \cos(\theta) = \frac{\vec{A} \cdot \vec{B}}{\|\vec{A}\| \|\vec{B}\|} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_i B_i}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} A_i^2} \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n} B_i^2}}$$

• Range: 0.0 (Completely Orthogonal / No Shared Words) to 1.0 (Identical Direction)

• Advantage: Angle-based metric remains unaffected by plot text length differences.

Comparison: TF-IDF vs. CountVectorizer (Bag of Words)

Feature TF-IDF Vectorizer CountVectorizer (Bag of Words)
Mathematical Approach Weights terms by frequency in document multiplied by log of inverse document frequency Simple count of term occurrences in the document
Handling Common Words Penalizes common generic words (e.g. "film", "story") across documents Gives higher magnitude to frequent words regardless of informativeness
Semantic Discrimination High (distinguishes rare keywords like "wormhole", "magician") Moderate (can be skewed by word repetitions)
Recommended Use Case Story & plot similarity, fine-grained semantic recommendation Quick keyword matching, uniform feature distributions
